Appendix · Architecture and AI guardrails

Where AI is allowed, and where it is not.

Deterministic engine owns
  • Every screening reference and threshold
  • Every score, sub-score and outcome
  • Confidence handling when inputs are missing
  • Traceability from each value to its citation
    • AI is permitted to
  • Extract text and tables from uploaded documents
  • Draft narrative prose from engine outputs
  • Answer questions about a completed assessment
    • AI is never permitted to
  • Set or adjust any screening reference
  • Decide a score, severity or outcome
  • Assert compliance, causation or health effect
    • Output linter: a banned-phrase check inspects every generated output and blocks compliance, causation and clinical language before it can leave the system.
